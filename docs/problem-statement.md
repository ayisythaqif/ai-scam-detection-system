#PROBLEM STATEMENT

Internet users may receive suspicious website links and phone calls or messages from unknown phone numbers. 
It can be difficult for users to determine whether these links or numbers are associated with scams before taking action. 
Existing checking methods may not always provide enough information about previously unreported threats, and users may mistake the absence of a warning for proof of safety. T
herefore, there is a need to investigate a web-based system that analyses 
suspicious URLs and checks available phone-number information to help users make more informed decisions.
