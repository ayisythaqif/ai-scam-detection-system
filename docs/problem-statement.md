### Problem Formulation

Internet users in Malaysia may encounter suspicious website URLs and phone numbers through messages, calls, social media, and other online communication channels. It can be difficult for users to determine whether these URLs or numbers are associated with scams before clicking a link, responding to a message, or making a payment. Although users can consult existing scam-report checking services, an unreported phone number or URL should not automatically be considered safe. Therefore, this project proposes a web-based system that uses machine learning to assess suspicious URLs and checks submitted phone numbers against available scam-report information to help users make more informed decisions.

**Target users:** Malaysian internet users who want to check suspicious URLs and phone numbers.

**Stakeholders:** Internet users, cybersecurity educators, researchers, and relevant authorities.

**Project scope:** URL risk classification, phone-number checks against an available and authorized data source, and displaying understandable results.

**Out of scope:** Identifying the private identity of a phone-number owner, guaranteeing scam detection, tracking callers, or automatically blocking calls.

**Evidence required:** Support the problem with credible research on phishing detection and official Malaysian guidance on scam checking. Cite the sources in the report.
